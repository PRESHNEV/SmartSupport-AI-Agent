ACCOUNT ACCESS
If you cannot sign in, check that the email address is correct and that Caps Lock is off. Use the password reset option on the sign-in page. If the account remains inaccessible, contact support and request an account-access ticket.

PASSWORD RESET
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address. Never share your password or one-time verification code.

ORDER TRACKING
Open the Orders page and select the relevant order to view its latest tracking status. If tracking has not updated, create a support ticket with the order reference.

RETURNS
Return eligibility and time limits depend on the seller's published policy and item category. Review the order's return information. This demo agent cannot approve refunds or returns.

CONTACT SUPPORT
If self-service steps do not resolve the issue, create a support ticket. Do not share passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.
